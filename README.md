# Hi, I'm Fabrizio Lallo 👋

**Founder · Freelance Software Engineer · Product Builder**

I'm a software engineer turned founder, passionate about building digital products at the intersection of technology, AI, and real-world problems.

I'm the founder of [TechCompenso](https://techcompenso.com), a platform focused on making the job market more transparent through salary data, career tools, and company insights.

Over the years, I've combined software engineering with product development, entrepreneurship, and content creation. I enjoy taking ideas from concept to production, experimenting with new technologies, and building products that solve real problems.

I'm particularly active on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fabrizio-lallo/), where I share insights on technology, salaries, careers, entrepreneurship, and the changing world of work.

## 🚀 Products I've built

- **[Candydati](https://github.com/fabrilallo/candydati)** — An open, self-hostable agentic ATS built for candidates, not just recruiters.
- **[XCompenso](https://xcompenso.com)** — A platform focused on salary transparency, career opportunities, and workplace insights for white-collar professionals in Italy.
- **[FuffAnnuncio](https://fuffannuncio.com)** — A tool designed to identify low-quality, misleading, and questionable job advertisements.
- **[Riccometro](https://riccometro.it)** — Find out whether you're rich or poor by comparing your income with that of other Italian taxpayers.
- **[RIC (RAL In Chiaro)](https://ralinchiaro.it)** — A searchable database of companies that disclose salary ranges (RAL) directly in their job advertisements.
- **[SuperTrumpTracker](https://supertrumptracker.com)** — A complete, searchable timeline of Donald Trump's posts on X and Truth Social.
